# Sentra Quick Scan

## 10 Questions

1. Is your OT network separated from your IT network?

2. Do you use industrial firewalls?

3. Is Multi-Factor Authentication enabled for remote OT access?

4. Do you have an up-to-date inventory of OT assets?

5. Are PLCs and SCADA systems continuously monitored?

6. Do you regularly apply security patches?

7. Do you have an OT Incident Response Plan?

8. Are backups tested periodically?

9. Do you follow IEC 62443 or another OT security framework?

10. Have you performed an OT cybersecurity assessment during the last 12 months?
