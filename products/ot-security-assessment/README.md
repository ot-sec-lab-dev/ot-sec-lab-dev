# OT Security Assessment

## Product

Professional OT Cybersecurity Assessment designed for industrial environments.

## Objective

Identify technical cybersecurity risks in Operational Technology environments and provide a prioritized remediation roadmap.

## Target customers

- Manufacturing
- Healthcare
- Water
- Energy
- Food
- Logistics
- Critical Infrastructure

## Deliverables

- Executive Summary
- OT Risk Score
- Architecture Review
- Purdue Assessment
- Asset Review
- Risk Matrix
- Top Risks
- Technical Recommendations
- Prioritized Roadmap
- Professional PDF Report

## Standards

- IEC 62443
- NIST SP 800-82
- MITRE ATT&CK for ICS
