# OT Security Assessment

A professional cybersecurity assessment for Operational Technology (OT) environments designed to identify security weaknesses, prioritize risks and provide a practical remediation roadmap.

- Manufacturing
- Healthcare
- Water
- Energy
- Food
- Logistics
- Critical Infrastructure

## What is included?

- OT architecture review
- Purdue model assessment
- Asset visibility review
- Network segmentation review
- Risk scoring
- Executive report
- Prioritized remediation roadmap
