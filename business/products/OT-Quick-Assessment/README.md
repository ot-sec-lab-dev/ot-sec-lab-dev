# OT Cybersecurity Quick Assessment

## Overview

The OT Cybersecurity Quick Assessment is the first commercial service offered by Sentra OT.

Its objective is to provide organisations with a rapid understanding of their Operational Technology cybersecurity posture through a structured assessment and a professional report.

---

## Target customers

- Hospitals
- Manufacturing
- Water utilities
- Energy companies
- Critical Infrastructure
- Industrial Integrators

---

## Duration

- 2-hour remote workshop
- Analysis
- Professional report delivered within 72 hours

---

## Deliverables

- Executive Summary
- OT Security Score (0–100)
- Top Risks
- Top Recommendations
- 90-Day Roadmap

---

## Assessment Areas

- Asset Inventory
- Network Segmentation
- Remote Access
- Patch Management
- Backup & Recovery
- OT Monitoring
- Incident Response
- Governance
- IEC 62443 Alignment
- Business Risk

---

## Goal

Generate a fast, professional and actionable cybersecurity assessment that serves as the entry point for future OT cybersecurity projects.
